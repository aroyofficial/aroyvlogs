THE PLAN

Our Puja Parikrama

Durga Puja in Kolkata is best experienced one neighbourhood at a time. For 2026, we are planning a six-day pandal-hopping journey across the city, combining iconic theme pujas, traditional community celebrations and some of the city's most atmospheric neighbourhoods.

The current route is built as a connected city circuit rather than a collection of isolated stops. Across the six days, we move through North Kolkata's heritage lanes, South Kolkata's major puja belt, Central Kolkata and the eastern neighbourhoods around Lake Town, Salt Lake, Newtown and Dumdum Park. Metro, local trains, buses, cabs and short walks are used to keep each day's movement practical.

Across the six planned days, we are targeting pandals: on Chaturthi, on Panchami, on Shashthi, on Saptami, on Astami and on Navami. The goal is to cover a wide range of neighbourhood pujas without turning the trip into a race, leaving room to eat, walk, photograph and actually enjoy the pandals along the way.

UNESCO HERITAGE

Tradition & History

THE ROOTS

From Zamindari Courtyards to the Streets

Durga Puja's history in Bengal traces back to late medieval times, originating as grand household celebrations hosted by wealthy landowners and aristocrats (Bonedi Barir Puja). By the early 20th century, the festival transitioned into public, community-funded events (Sarbojanin), turning the streets of Kolkata into a massive, democratic art gallery accessible to everyone, regardless of class or creed.

In 2021, UNESCO inscribed Kolkata's Durga Puja on its Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, recognizing its unique blend of art, community bonding, and craft traditions.

THE ARTISTRY

Crafting the Divine & the Ephemeral

At the heart of the festival is the extraordinary craftsmanship. Months before the autumn festival, clay modelers in Kumartuli sculpt exquisite idols of Goddess Durga and her children from sacred Ganga clay. Parallelly, architects and decorators build elaborate, thematic temporary structures (pandals) using bamboo, fabric, wood, and recycled materials, showcasing breathtaking creativity that is dismantled after the five-day carnival.

The air during the festival is thick with the sound of traditional Dhak drums, the aroma of burning incense (dhunuchi), and local delicacies that define Kolkata's street food culture.

PANDALS · DAYS

Zones we are targeting

North Kolkata is the beating heart of Durga Puja, and this year, we are going absolutely all out! We've mapped out a trail that's honestly insane - a massive mix of timeless heritage, iconic para pujas, and those crowd-favourite theme pandals we've been dying to see. We'll be diving deep into the historic lanes of Kumartuli, Bagbazar, Sovabazar, Ahiritola, Hatkhola, and Jorabagan, soaking in the electric festive energy of Shyambazar, Hatibagan, Chorbagan, and Gouri Bari, and cutting across Tala, Belgachia, Maniktala, and Ultadanga. And we're not stopping there - we're also hitting the massive visual spectacles across Baranagar, Dum Dum, Dum Dum Park, and Baguiati.

Central Kolkata adds a vibrant mix of heritage, culture, and festive energy to our citywide Durga Puja trail. As we move between different parts of Kolkata, we'll be stopping at the pandals and neighbourhoods of Bowbazar, College Street, Entally, and Park Street along the way. These locations will be woven into our overall route through short walks and convenient public transport, allowing us to keep exploring without treating Central Kolkata as one separate circuit.

South Kolkata is honestly where we are spending the most time this Puja, and for good reason - the sheer scale of what we've planned is absolutely wild! We're not cramming it all into one day though; we've sprinkled these stops across different outings, mixing them in with our North and Central trails so every single day feels fresh. We'll be chasing the iconic pandals around Kalighat, Chetla, Bhowanipore, Hazra, and Alipore, then jumping straight into the buzzing energy of Ballygunge, Gariahat, Dhakuria, Bosepukur, and Selimpur. From there, we're soaking in the lakeside charm around Rabindra Sarobar, Jodhpur Park, New Alipore, and Tollygunge, before pushing deep south into the mega-pandal territory of Garia, Santoshpur, Jadavpur, Naktala, Patuli, Kudghat, Mudiali, and Rajdanga. Landmark pujas, hidden neighbourhood gems, and a ridiculous number of food stops in between - South Kolkata is where the real marathon happens, and trust me, we're here for every single bit of it!

Port Area centered right around Khidirpur, brings a completely different and refreshing flavour to our Puja plans! Instead of endless cross-city running, we carved out this compact dockside pocket to slow things down just a bit and explore at our own pace. We'll be weaving through the vibrant, tight-knit community pandals tucked away inside Khidirpur's historic lanes. It's an intimate, hidden-gem stretch that we've slotted into our itinerary - far less transit madness, great local street food, and a unique side of Kolkata's Puja that hits completely differently!

East Kolkata is where the game completely changes, and we are honestly most hyped about this stretch! We've mixed these stops across different days alongside our other zones, so it never feels like a detour - it feels like a whole new experience every time we swing east. We'll be kicking things off in the buzzing, old-school neighbourhood lanes of Beliaghata, Kankurgachi, and Nager Bazar, where the para puja warmth hits you right in the feels. And then - total gear shift - we're jumping straight into the jaw-dropping, larger-than-life theme pandals of Salt Lake, Lake Town, and New Town, where the installations are so massive they'll literally make your neck hurt from looking up! From tight, heartfelt community celebrations to grand, city-scale spectacles under open skies, East Kolkata gives us the best of both worlds, and we're soaking in every single bit of it!

South West Kolkata is where the real artistic heavyweights come alive, and honestly, we couldn't imagine our Puja trail without this zone! We've woven these stops into our different days because the level of creative madness here definitely deserves its own spotlight. We'll be diving straight into the powerhouse hubs of Behala and Haridevpur, where the theme concepts consistently blow everyone's minds year after year, and winding our way through the buzzing neighbourhood lanes of Paschim Putiary and Thakurpukur to catch some of the grandest visual spectacles in the entire city. The sheer passion, craftsmanship, and electric crowd energy in this corner of Kolkata are unmatched - it's pure, unfiltered Puja magic!

GUIDE FOR OTHERS

How to Follow this Parikrama

If you are planning to follow or adapt these itineraries for your own Puja walk or vlog, here are a few practical tips designed to make the experience smooth and enjoyable:

⏱️ Timing is Everything

Our itineraries start around 7:30 AM or 9:30 AM to balance crowd levels. For the best photography and shortest lines, start early in the morning. If you want to experience the dramatic lighting, plan your walks for late evening, but prepare for heavy crowds.

🚇 Transit Strategy

Skip ticket lines by getting a Metro Smart Card. When transitioning between zones, use the metro network where practical. For short hops or late-night transfers, use a bus, auto-rickshaw, cab or local yellow taxi.

📸 Vlogging & Etiquette

Keep your gear light. A smartphone or a compact mirrorless camera on a gimbal works best in crowded lanes. Be respectful of worshippers inside the inner sanctum of the pandals where photography is sometimes restricted.

🍲 Pandal Food Crawl

Puja and food are inseparable. The route already includes planned lunch stops, while temporary food stalls near major pandals are useful for quick breaks between longer walking sections.
