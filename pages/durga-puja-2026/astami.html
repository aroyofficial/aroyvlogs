19 OCTOBER · MONDAY

Astami

Meet-up: 4:00 PM · Ramrajatala Station

🔀 Itinerary: Ramrajatala → Khidirpur → Bally
🚶 Total Walking Distance: 2300 m ~ 2.5 km (approx 50 min)
🎪 Total Pandals: 4
🗺️ Zones Covered: Port Area
  1. Transfer: 🚕 Take a cab from Ramrajatala Station to Khidirpur Manasa Mandir.
  2. 01
    KHIDIRPUR (PORT AREA)

    25 Pally

    🚶 150 m · ⏱️ 3 min from Khidirpur Manasa Mandir.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 82nd year, this long-running Khidirpur community Puja boasts a rich history of thematic installations dating back to 1945. The 2026 presentation features the theme 'Durga Bandana' (দুর্গা বন্দনা), brought to life by creative artists Sujit Das and Buddhadeb Karmakar.
  3. 02
    KHIDIRPUR (PORT AREA)

    74 Pally

    🚶 550 m · ⏱️ 11 min from 25 Pally.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 71st year, this puja is known for its long tradition of community-led thematic work and traditional craftsmanship. The 2026 presentation features the theme 'Porlo Pujo 71 e, Nojor Rakhun 74 e' (পড়ল পুজো ৭১ এ, নজর রাখুন ৭৪ এ), brought to life by a creative team including Iman Chandra, Akash Chakraborty, Shuvam Banerjee, Avik Sen, Sudip Sanyal, and Pallab Sekhar Das.
  4. 03
    KHIDIRPUR (PORT AREA)

    75 Pally

    🚶 650 m · ⏱️ 13 min from 74 Pally.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 63rd year, the 2026 presentation features the theme 'Parasray - Ek Chironton Satya' (পরাশ্রয় - এক চিরন্তন সত্য). The concept draws on the koel's brood-parasitic behaviour to explore human exploitation of nature and the wider ecological balance, brought to life by a creative team including Pradipta Karmakar, Parimal Pal, Soumen Haldar, Koustav Biswas, Azaan Chakraborty, and Paresh Bera.
  5. 04
    KHIDIRPUR (PORT AREA)

    Khidderpore Pally Sharadiya

    🚶 350 m · ⏱️ 7 min from 75 Pally.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 85th year, this established Khidirpur puja presents a nostalgic tribute to Kolkata's city heritage Tram. The 2026 presentation features the theme 'Chol Jabo Toke Niye' (চল যাবো তোকে নিয়ে), brought to life by a creative team including Dipankar Dey, Soumen Pal, Ashu Chakraborty, and Debajyoti Jana.
  6. Transfer: 🚶 Walk 600 m · ⏱️ about 12 min to Fancy Market and take a cab to Bally Station.
