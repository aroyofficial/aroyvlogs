18 OCTOBER · SUNDAY

Saptami

Meet-up: 9:00 AM · Bally Station

🔀 Itinerary: Bally → Howrah → Ballygunge → Gariahat → Sealdah → Baranagar → Bally Halt.
🚶 Total Walking Distance: 8270 m ~ 8.5 km (approx 2 hour 50 min)
🎪 Total Pandals: 10
🗺️ Zones Covered: South Kolkata, North Kolkata.
  1. Transfer: Train from Bally Station to Howrah Junction, then bus to Rashbehari Crossing.
  2. 01
    BALLYGUNGE (SOUTH KOLKATA)

    Deshapriya Park

    Walk about 850 m from Rashbehari Crossing.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 89th year, the 2026 presentation takes the form of a fictional temple, featuring multiple spires, a grand staircase and classical architectural detailing. The idol is being created by Sanatan Rudra Pal, with a special lighting presentation planned to add to the visual experience.
  3. 02
    BALLYGUNGE (SOUTH KOLKATA)

    Tridhara Sammilani

    Walk about 600 m.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 80th year, the 2026 presentation will feature a theme based on Tibet. Known for its distinctive South Kolkata style blending tradition with contemporary artistic expression, further theme details will be officially announced soon by the Puja Committee.
  4. 03
    BALLYGUNGE (SOUTH KOLKATA)

    Ballygunge Cultural Association

    Walk about 1 km.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 76th year, the 2026 presentation features the theme 'Connection' (যোজন). Known for its distinctive South Kolkata style blending tradition with contemporary artistic expression, the concept is led by artist Sushanta Shibani Paul, supported by a creative team including Adhir Pal, Lakshman Roy, Suman Maity, Shirsak Paul, Samir Mondal, Tapan Maji, Gopal Mondal, Bikas Sardar, Tuhin Das, Nepal Maity, Goutam Das, and Tokan Das.
  5. 04
    BALLYGUNGE (SOUTH KOLKATA)

    Samaj Sebi Sangha

    Walk about 120 m.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 81st year, the Puja pays tribute to Uttam Kumar's birth centenary through 'Mahapujoy Mahanayak' (মহাপুজোয় মহানায়ক). The presentation recreates the nostalgia of 1970s-80s Kolkata, bringing together film posters, photographs, memorabilia and stories from the legendary actor's life. Bishwanath Dey is the theme-maker, with the idol by Arighna Saha.
  6. 05
    LUNCH OPTIONS

    Zeeshan or Sorshe Posto

    Choose one of the planned lunch stops.

    Zeeshan ↗ Sorshe Posto ↗
  7. 06
    GARIAHAT (SOUTH KOLKATA)

    Hindustan Park

    Walk about 1.1 km.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 96th year, the 2026 presentation features the theme 'Spark' (স্ফুলিঙ্গ), placing emphasis on Bengali craftsmanship and intricate handwork. The installation is brought to life by a creative team including Malay Subhomoy, Premendu Bikash Chaki, and Dipamoy Das.
  8. 07
    GARIAHAT (SOUTH KOLKATA)

    Gariahat Hindustan Club

    Walk about 450 m.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 64th year, this long-running Gariahat Puja continues its history of traditional and colourful artistic presentations. The 2026 presentation features the theme 'Durga Dalan' (দুর্গা দালান), conceptualised by creative artist Raju Sarkar, supported by lighting design from Ashok Das and Chitralekha Pal, and decorations by Golden Decorator.
  9. 08
    GARIAHAT (SOUTH KOLKATA)

    Singhi Park

    Walk about 700 m.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 85th year, this established Gariahat Puja maintains a strong community and traditional identity. The 2026 presentation features the theme 'Bostario' (বস্তারীয়), conceptualised by Ankita Bandyopadhyay, with an idol crafted by Pradip Rudra Pal and a background score by Subhen Chattopadhyay.
  10. 09
    GARIAHAT (SOUTH KOLKATA)

    Ekdalia Evergreen

    Walk about 350 m.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 84th year, the Puja retains its iconic Sabeki idol while taking inspiration from Gujarat's magnificent Somnath Temple for the 2026 pandal. The temple-inspired architecture provides the year's major visual attraction, while the traditional idol remains at the heart of the Puja.
  11. Transfer: 🚶 550 m · ⏱️ 11 min to Ballygunge Junction then take a train to Sealdah Junction. Then take any train to Dum Dum Junction. Then take a bus to Sinthir More.
  12. 09
    BARANAGAR (NORTH KOLKATA)

    Bandhudal Sporting Club

    Walk about 150 m from Sinthir More.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 65th year, Bandhudal Sporting Club presents the 2026 theme Lotus Temple. The creative concept is envisioned by Shibsankar Das and executed by Bidyut Pal, featuring an idol crafted by Mintu Pal.
  13. 09
    BARANAGAR (NORTH KOLKATA)

    Barangar Netaji Colony Lowland

    Walk about 950 m.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 5th year, this relatively young but rapidly rising Durga Puja in Baranagar is already making its mark on the local circuit. While the final 2026 theme is not yet confirmed, ongoing preparations reveal beautiful and intricate plywood structures currently taking shape.
  14. Transfer: 🚶 650 m · ⏱️ 13 min to Palpara Bus Stop then take a bus to Bally Halt.
