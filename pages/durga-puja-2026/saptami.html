18 OCTOBER · SUNDAY

Saptami

Meet-up: 9:00 AM · Bally Station

Itinerary: Bally → Howrah → Rashbehari Crossing → Ballygunge → Gariahat → South Kolkata → Ballygunge Station → Sealdah → Bally.
  1. Transfer: Train from Bally Station to Howrah Junction, then bus to Rashbehari Crossing.
  2. 01
    SOUTH KOLKATA

    Deshapriya Park

    Walk about 850 m from Rashbehari Crossing.

    Google Maps ↗Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 89th year, the 2026 presentation takes the form of a fictional white-and-gold temple, featuring multiple spires, a grand staircase and classical architectural detailing. The idol is being created by Padma Shri Sanatan Rudra Pal, with a special lighting presentation planned to add to the visual experience.
  3. 02
    SOUTH KOLKATA

    Tridhara Sammilani

    Walk about 600 m.

    Google Maps ↗Toilets ↗
    Known for blending tradition with contemporary artistic expression, the 2026 presentation is described as a fusion of sculptural work and modern lighting. The committee is continuing its distinctive South Kolkata style, though a reliable final theme title and artist credit have not yet been confirmed.
  4. 03
    BALLYGUNGE

    Ballygunge Cultural Association

    Walk about 1 km.

    Google Maps ↗Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 76th year, the 2026 theme is “Yojon” (যোজন), exploring the connections between people, generations, Bengali culture, art and the environment. Artist Sushanta Shivani Pal leads the creative vision, while the project also focuses strongly on sustainability through recycling festival materials and turning waste into reusable products.
  5. 04
    BALLYGUNGE

    Samaj Sebi Sangha

    Walk about 120 m.

    Google Maps ↗Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 81st year, the Puja pays tribute to Uttam Kumar's birth centenary through “Mahapujoy Mahanayak”. The presentation recreates the nostalgia of 1970s-80s Kolkata, bringing together film posters, photographs, memorabilia and stories from the legendary actor's life. Bishwanath Dey is the theme-maker, with the idol by Arighna Saha.

    SOUTH KOLKATA

    Ekdalia Evergreen

    Walk about 350 m.

    Google Maps ↗Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 84th year, the Puja retains its iconic Sabeki idol while taking inspiration from Gujarat's magnificent Somnath Temple for the 2026 pandal. The temple-inspired architecture provides the year's major visual attraction, while the traditional idol remains at the heart of the Puja.
