17 OCTOBER · SATURDAY

Shashthi

Meet-up: 9:00 AM · Sonarpur Junction

🔀 Itinerary: Sonarpur → Bagbazar → Shyambazar → Kumartuli → Hatkhola → Ahiritola → Chorbagan → Maniktala → Gouribari → Hatibagan → Sovabazar → Sonarpur.
🚶 Total Walking Distance: 9740 m ~ 10 km (approx 3 hour 20 min)
🎪 Total Pandals: 18
🗺️ Zones Covered: North Kolkata
  1. Transfer: Start at Sonarpur Junction. Take a cab to the Bagbazar Puja area.
  2. 01
    BAGBAZAR (NORTH KOLKATA)

    Bagbazar Sarbojanin

    Starting point of the North Kolkata circuit.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 108th year, this iconic heritage puja is renowned for its traditional Sabeki idol and timeless cultural significance. Eschewing modern themes, it continues to preserve the authentic spirit and grandeur of Kolkata's classic Durga Puja celebrations.
  3. 02
    SHYAMBAZAR (NORTH KOLKATA)

    Jagat Mukherjee Park

    🚶 750 m · ⏱️ 15 min from Bagbazar Sarbojanin.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 90th year, this North Kolkata puja features the theme 'Shankar Nama' (শঙ্কর নামা), narrated by Avijit Ghatak, with an idol crafted by Amal Paul.
  4. 03
    KUMARTULI (NORTH KOLKATA)

    Kumartuli Sarbojanin

    🚶 500 m · ⏱️ 10 min from Jagat Mukherjee Park.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 96th year, the 2026 presentation features the theme 'Mon Diye Dekhun' (মন দিয়ে দেখুন), conceptualised by Manas Roy, with an idol crafted by Dipankar Pal, lighting by S B Electric, and pandal decoration by S B Decorator.
  5. 04
    KUMARTULI (NORTH KOLKATA)

    Kumartuli Park

    🚶 300 m · ⏱️ 6 min from Kumartuli Sarbojanin.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 34th year, the 2026 presentation features the theme 'Gangotri theke Ganga' (গঙ্গোত্রী থেকে গঙ্গা), tracing the journey of the Ganga from its Himalayan origin towards the Bay of Bengal. The concept, brought to life by Subol Pal, gives the pandal a strong connection with India's cultural and spiritual relationship with the river.
  6. 05
    HATKHOLA (NORTH KOLKATA)

    Hatkhola Gosaipara Sarbojanin

    🚶 250 m · ⏱️ 5 min from Kumartuli Park.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 90th year, this neighbourhood puja in the historic North Kolkata belt is known for its local Puja atmosphere and place within the Kumortuli-Hatkhola circuit. The 2026 presentation features the theme 'Punoragomon' (পুনরাগমন), conceptualised by Ajit Pal.
  7. 06
    AHIRITOLA (NORTH KOLKATA)

    Ahiritola Sarbojanin

    🚶 800 m · ⏱️ 16 min from Hatkhola Gosaipara Sarbojanin.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 87th year, the 2026 presentation follows a Sabeki/traditional direction with the theme 'Shoroter Prothom Spondon' (শরতের প্রথম স্পন্দন). The concept and creation are handled by Manas Das, featuring an idol crafted by Subrata Mridha and lighting designed by Soumen Chakraborty.
  8. 07
    AHIRITOLA (NORTH KOLKATA)

    Ahiritola Jubak Brinda

    🚶 350 m · ⏱️ 7 min from Ahiritola Sarbojanin.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 56th year, this long-running Ahiritola puja features the theme 'Sesh Belay Rabir Alo' (শেষ বেলায় রবির আলো), with art direction by Abhijit Ghatak and research by Susobhan Adhikari. The 2026 presentation is brought to life by a dedicated team including Somnath, Sudarshan, Sukumar, Samir, Bapi, Baburam, Sumon, and Prasanta.
  9. Transfer: 🚶 600 m · ⏱️ 12 min to Sovabazar Metro Station. Then take a bus to Jorasanko Thakur Bari.

    HATIBAGAN (NORTH KOLKATA)

    Hatibagan Nabinpally

    🚶 350 m · ⏱️ 7 min from North Tridhara.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 93rd year, this long-running Hatibagan-area puja forms an integral part of the traditional North Kolkata circuit. The 2026 presentation features the theme 'Hashi Khushi Pujo - Choto Thakar Boro Moja' (হাসি খুশি পুজো - ছোট থাকার বড় মজা), conceptualised by Shankar Pal, with an idol crafted by Arighna Saha, lighting by Dipankar Dey, and music by Gautam Ghosh.
  22. 17
    SOVABAZAR (NORTH KOLKATA)

    Sovabazar Rajbari

    🚶 850 m · ⏱️ 17 min from Hatibagan Nabinpally.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 269th year, this iconic bonedi bari (household) puja was established in 1757 by Raja Nabakrishna Deb. Held in the grand Nat Mandap, it is renowned for its traditional Ek-chala sabeki idol, ancient family rituals, and a rich heritage that has hosted historical figures from Lord Clive to Rabindranath Tagore.
