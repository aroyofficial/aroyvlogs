    NORTH KOLKATA

    Belgachia Sadharon Durgotsav

    🚶 550 m · ⏱️ 11 min from Belgachia Metro Station.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 79th year in 2026, one of North Kolkata’s traditional community pujas combines neighbourhood festive culture with a contemporary pandal presentation. Artist Anirban Das brings to life the 2026 theme, 'Unbound' (বন্ধনমুক্ত).
  3. 02
    NORTH KOLKATA

    Tala Pratyay

    🚶 400 m · ⏱️ 8 min from Belgachia Sadharon Durgotsav.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 101st year in 2026, this puja is known for treating Durga Puja as an ambitious form of public art. Artist Susanta Shibani Paul is leading the 2026 artistic work, featuring the newly announced theme, 'Detachment' (বিয়োজন).
  4. Transfer: 🚶 200 m · ⏱️ 4 min to Tala Park Circus Maidan → Then take an auto to Tala Station.
  5. 03
    NORTH KOLKATA

    Tala Barowari

    🚶 550 m · ⏱️ 11 min from Tala Station.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 106th year in 2026, Tala Barowari presents the theme 'Barowarir Durgabari' (বারোয়ারির দুর্গাবাড়ি), envisioned by artist Prathama Prashant Paul. The puja continues its "Uttorer Uttor" legacy with a simple, relatable concept that prioritizes the sacred space of the Goddess over complex abstractions. The pandal's design is inspired by the manifestation of Adishakti (primal energy) as described in Hindu scriptures, aiming to evoke pure devotion while showcasing India's rich traditional art and culture to the world.
  6. Transfer: 🚶 550 m · ⏱️ 11 min to Tala Station → then take an auto/bus to Belgachia Metro Station → take a metro to Rabindra Sarobar Metro Station.

    SOUTH KOLKATA

    Mudiali Club

    🚶 290 m · ⏱️ 6 min from Rabindra Sarobar Metro Station.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Dating back to 1935, this puja reaches its 92nd year in 2026. Known for its artistic approach, distinctive idol, and carefully designed pandal environments, the 2026 presentation features the theme 'Traditional Folktales' (ঐতিহ্যের লোককথা) - Timeless Tales, Timeless Devotion, conceptualized by artist Biman Saha.
  8. 05
    SOUTH KOLKATA

    Shib Mandir Sarbojonin

    🚶 400 m · ⏱️ 8 min from Mudiali Club.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 90th year, this South Kolkata neighbourhood puja brings together traditional community worship and contemporary pandal decoration. Directed by artist Prashant Paul, the 2026 presentation features the theme 'Festival' (পরব). The extensive creative team bringing this 90-year heritage to life includes research by Shubhojit Santra, the idol by Tapan Majhi, background music by Sujoy Rudra, lighting design by Premendu Bikash Chaki and Sushanta Haldar, and pandal structure by Maa Kali Decorator.

    🚶 600 m · ⏱️ 12 min from Mudiali Club.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 97th year, this South Kolkata neighbourhood puja is known for its local festive atmosphere and thematic presentation, fitting naturally into the Chakraberia–Ballygunge circuit. The 2026 theme is 'Sharad Natyatsav' (শারদ নাট্যোৎসব), directed by Bimal Samanta. The creative team features the idol by Amal Pal, lighting by Premendu Bikash Chaki, and background music by Ashu Chakraborty.
