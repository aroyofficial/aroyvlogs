    NORTH KOLKATA

    Tala Pratyay

    Walk about 800 m from Tala Park.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 101st year in 2026, this puja is known for treating Durga Puja as an ambitious form of public art. Artist Susanta Shibani Paul is leading the 2026 artistic work, while the final theme is being kept under wraps.

    SOUTH KOLKATA

    Mudiali Club

    Walk about 290 m from Rabindra Sarobar Metro Station.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Dating back to 1935, this puja reaches its 92nd year in 2026. It is particularly known for its artistic approach, distinctive idol and carefully designed pandal environments. Final 2026 theme and creative-team details are not yet reliably announced.
  6. 04
    SOUTH KOLKATA

    Shib Mandir Sarbojonin

    🚶 400 m · ⏱️ 8 min from Mudiali Club.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    This South Kolkata neighbourhood puja brings together traditional community worship and contemporary pandal decoration. It is a convenient stop in the Rabindra Sarobar–South Kolkata circuit. Official 2026 theme and artist details are yet to be reliably confirmed.
  7. 05
    SOUTH KOLKATA

    Suruchi Sangha

    Walk about 1.7 km from Mudiali Club.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 74th year in 2026, this is one of South Kolkata’s best-known thematic pujas. The 2026 theme is “Padma” (Lotus), exploring the cultural and symbolic significance of the lotus in Indian artistic and spiritual traditions.
  8. 06
    SOUTH KOLKATA

    Chetla Agrani

    Walk about 1.4 km from Suruchi Sangha.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    The 2026 celebration marks its 67th year, continuing a long-running South Kolkata community tradition known for large-scale thematic presentations. The final 2026 theme and artist details are yet to be reliably confirmed.
  9. 07
    SOUTH KOLKATA

    Alipore Sarbajonin

    🚶 650 m · ⏱️ 13 min from Chetla Agrani.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Celebrating its 81st year in 2026, this year’s concept draws from the Ramayana through Sita’s perspective. Rather than focusing only on Rama’s journey, the presentation seeks to bring Sita’s voice and viewpoint into focus.
  10. Transfer: 🚶 140 m · ⏱️ 3 min to Alipore Chetla Crossing → take a bus to Keoratala Samshan.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗

    CENTRAL KOLKATA

    College Square

    Walk about 850 m from Central Metro Station.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    Famous for its spectacular illumination reflected across the adjoining water body, this is one of Kolkata’s most visually distinctive Durga Puja destinations. The combination of the illuminated pandal, lake and historic surroundings creates one of the city’s signature Puja-night scenes. Final 2026 theme and artist details are still awaiting reliable confirmation.

    CENTRAL KOLKATA

    Santosh Mitra Square

    Walk about 1.3 km from College Square.

    Google Maps ↗ Toilets ↗
    The 2026 theme is “Sanatani Chetanay Vande Mataram”. Artist Shibashankar Das is responsible for the thematic design, while Mintu Paul is creating the idol. The puja is known for ambitious installations and large-scale visual storytelling.
