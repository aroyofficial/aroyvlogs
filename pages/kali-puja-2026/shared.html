PLAN THE EVENING

Keep the route flexible

What to check on the day

  • Police and municipal traffic diversions, entry and exit arrangements.
  • Railway notices and the last suitable service for your return.
  • Organiser updates for puja timings, queue management and photography.

Carry a simple plan

  • Save the arrival station, a group meeting point and your return option.
  • Bring water, a charged phone and only the gear you can manage in a crowd.
  • Allow time for queues and use marked walkways and local instructions.
