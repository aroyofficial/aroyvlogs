A BENGAL PERSPECTIVE

One festival night, many local traditions

BHUT CHATURDASHI

Fourteen lamps for the night

The evening before Kali Puja is Bhut Chaturdashi, when many Bengali families light fourteen lamps, or choddoprodeep, and prepare a meal of fourteen leafy greens, known as choddoshak. These customs honour ancestors and mark the threshold of the festival night.

KALI PUJA / SHYAMA PUJA

Worship through the night

On the new-moon night, Bengalis worship Maa Kali as homes and neighbourhoods glow with lamps. Community pujas bring together devotion, elaborate pandals and the shared excitement of exploring the town after dark.

DEEPAVALI / DIWALI

A festival of light across India

Kali Puja coincides with Deepavali, also called Diwali, though the festival takes different forms across India. In Bengal, the night pairs Kali worship with the glow of lamps, bringing a distinctive local expression to the wider celebration of light.
